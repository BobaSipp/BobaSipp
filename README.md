From crafting backend logic to breathing life into the frontend, I treat every project as a canvas. Whether it's a CLI tool in Python or a real-time 3D experience in the browser, I bring the same pixel-perfect, boba-fueled energy to the table.

## ✨ Specialization: 3D Web Experiences

What truly gets me hyped is **immersive 3D animation on the web**. I'm deeply passionate about creating interactive, visually rich experiences that push the boundaries of what a browser can do.

**My 3D & animation toolkit includes:**
- 🟢 **Three.js** — 3D scenes, lighting, and geometry
- ⚡ **WebGL** — Low-level graphics & shader programming
- 🌀 **GSAP** — Silky-smooth timeline animations
- 🎨 **Spline** — Rapid 3D design & prototyping

I believe the web is the ultimate creative medium — and I'm here to make it move, glow, and feel alive.
